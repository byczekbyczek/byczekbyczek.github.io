What responsible coaching needs

Useful feedback should remain smaller than the evidence supporting it.

  • 01Structured facts firstParse and normalize the timeline before any generated explanation.
  • 02Visibility-aware claimsNever invent hidden opponents or a complete battlefield state.
  • 03Few high-impact momentsPrioritize decisions that changed options, positioning, or team support.
  • 04No fake counterfactual certaintyAlternative routes remain suggestions unless a validated model supports more.
Evidence before promises

The concept becomes a product one verified layer at a time.

The immediate goal is not a public upload box. It is proving what authorized replay files contain, how reliably they can be parsed, and which conclusions the data can actually support.

01

Parser feasibility

Validate archive structure, versions, limits, failure behavior, and obtainable fields on authorized samples.

02

Deterministic 2D MVP

Render verified results, routes, visible events, and a synchronized timeline before adding AI.

03

Measured estimates

Train and calibrate a model on a suitable dataset, report uncertainty, and test failure cases.

04

Evidence-linked coach

Generate concise guidance only from structured facts, with timestamps and inspectable support.

Current status: Replay Lab is a research direction and interactive design study. The shipping BlitzPredict development alpha remains a local pre-battle roster workflow with explicit one-shot capture and a file fallback.
